01. Background & Philosophy

About Me

A 4th-year Computer Engineering student dedicated to combining physical computing, spatial design, and software systems into functional real-world solutions.

I am a Computer Engineering student based in Antipolo City, Rizal. My academic journey centers on exploring how hardware, code, and spatial thinking converge to solve practical challenges.

Throughout my coursework and personal exploration, I have worked across diverse domains—from writing embedded C++ firmware for assistive microcontroller devices to drafting structured cabling blueprints in AutoCAD and visualizing 3D architectural spaces in Twinmotion.

I approach learning with curiosity, precision, and an appetite for hands-on experimentation. Rather than confining myself to one silo, I value understanding the complete lifecycle: the hardware constraints, the computational logic, and the visual clarity of the final product.

Current Focus & Exploration
Deepening my knowledge of full-stack development, IoT wireless protocols, modern frontend systems, and preparing for graduation capstone deliverables.

Embedded Systems & Hardware

CAD & 3D Spatial Design
